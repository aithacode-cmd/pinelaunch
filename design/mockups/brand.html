PineLaunch — logo concepts

Three directions, all built on a 24-unit grid, all working at 16 px. Concept A is the recommendation: it reads as a pine first and a launch second, and its silhouette stays clear in a favicon.

Concept ARecommended

Pine on the pad

Two tiers and a trunk make the pine. The bar underneath is the launch pad, and the small gap means the tree has already lifted off. Calm, solid, unmistakably a pine.

Concept B

Rocket pine

One tall triangle with a notch cut into its base, so the outline is a pine and a rocket with fins at the same time. Sharper and more energetic, slightly less friendly.

Concept C

P monogram

A stem and a sideways pine form a letter P that also reads as a play or launch arrow. Clever, but it loses the pine at small sizes and looks like a media button.
