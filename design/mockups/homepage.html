Who it is for

You do not need to know how to code. You need one small idea and two or three hours a week.

Students 13 to 17

Most have never written code. The guides assume you are starting from zero.

Beginners are welcome

You learn by building one thing, not by finishing a course first.

People with a small idea

An app, a website, a tool, or a one-screen game. Something one real person you know would use.

Three weeks, three jobs

Each week has one goal. Finish it and the next one is waiting.

What you leave with

One working project

Built by you, used by real people.

A public link

Anyone can open it. It is yours to keep.

Feedback from real people

Three testers watched, and you fixed what mattered.

A way of working

Scope small, build the ugly version, test, fix one thing, ship.

Who runs this

PineLaunch is built and run by a high school student, with his parents' support. A parent is in the group chat. The program is free during the pilot.

This site keeps no accounts and collects no personal information. Your progress is saved in your own browser.

Start with one small idea.

The idea picker takes about ten minutes and gives you a Project Card. That is the whole first step.
