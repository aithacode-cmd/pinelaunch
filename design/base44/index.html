01Who it is for
Fit check

A program for beginners with one small idea

You do not need to know how to code. You need one small idea and two or three hours a week for three weeks.

  • 01

    Students 13 to 17

    Most have never written code. The guides assume you are starting from zero.

  • 02

    Beginners are welcome

    You learn by building one thing, not by finishing a course first.

  • 03

    People with a small idea

    An app, a website, a tool, or a one-screen game. Something one real person you know would use.

Who it is not for
  • Not a coding bootcamp
  • Not a certificate
  • Not a competition

If you want a finished polished product in a weekend, this is the wrong shape. Three weeks is what makes the first version possible.

03Possible projects
What you could build

Four kinds of project fit in three weeks

These are illustrative examples, not student projects. Each one is deliberately small enough to finish.

Pick the kind of thing you already open every day. A smaller version of a familiar object is easier to finish than a brand new idea.

04The outcome
What you leave with

Four things that exist after Dec 20

Not a certificate and not a grade. A working project, a public link, notes from real testers, and a way of working you can reuse.

  • One working project

    Built by you, used by real people.

  • A public link

    Anyone can open it. It is yours to keep.

  • Feedback from real people

    Three testers watched, and you fixed what mattered.

  • A way of working

    Scope small, build the ugly version, test, fix one thing, ship.

PLProgram leadPineLaunch · 2026

This site keeps no accounts and collects no personal information. Progress is saved in your own browser, and nothing is uploaded.

  • Discord

    One channel per week. Ask a question when you are stuck for more than twenty minutes.

  • Weekly form

    Three questions at the end of each week. It takes two minutes and replaces a long survey.

  • A parent in the chat

    A parent is in the group chat for the whole cohort. Nobody messages students privately.
