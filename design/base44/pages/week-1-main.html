Idea

Dec 1 to 6

Choose something small enough to finish. Seven questions give you a Project Card.

Steps

  1. 15 pts
    What to do
    • Write down one real person: a brother, a teammate, a friend, a coach.
    • Next to the name, write one thing that person does every week that takes too long or gets forgotten.
    • Choose the smallest problem on that list. Not the most impressive one.
    How to know it is done

    You can say the person's name and the problem in one short sentence.

    If you are stuck

    If nobody comes to mind, look at your own week. The problem you repeat most is a good start. A family member is always allowed.

  2. 15 pts
    What to do
    • Finish this sentence: this helps one person do one thing in less time.
    • Keep one job only. If you write two jobs, keep the one your person needs first.
    • Read the sentence out loud. If it needs explaining, it is too big.
    How to know it is done

    Your sentence fits in one line and starts with a verb.

    If you are stuck

    Try starting with keep track of, remind, show, or add up. Those four verbs cover most small projects.

    Locked example

    Example · One job, written small opens after you try this step yourself. No answer is checked or sent anywhere.

    0 of 2 sentences
  3. 20 pts
    What to do
    • List three things the first version must do. Three is the limit, not a target.
    • For each feature, ask: does my one person need this to finish the job? If not, cut it.
    • Move every other idea to a note called later. It stays there for now.
    How to know it is done

    Three features are written down, and every extra idea sits in a later list.

    If you are stuck

    A feature is a thing the person can do, not a screen you want to build. Add a task. Tick a task. See what is due.

  4. 10 pts
    What to do
    • Use two plain words, easy to type and easy to say out loud.
    • Avoid numbers, punctuation, and names that need a story to understand.
    • Say it to someone. If they repeat it back correctly, keep it.
    How to know it is done

    You can spell the name for someone without stopping.

    If you are stuck

    Describe what it holds: Homework, Practice, Club. Short and boring is fine. A working name is not a permanent one.

  5. 15 pts
    What to do
    • Open the idea picker and answer its seven questions.
    • Copy the finished card and paste it into the Discord channel for week one.
    • Submit the week one form.
    How to know it is done

    The card is saved in your browser and posted in Discord.

    If you are stuck

    If you get stuck on a question, answer it roughly. The card can be rebuilt in ten minutes later.

Prompts for this week

Hints and small steps only

Copy a prompt into ChatGPT and paste your Project Card details where the brackets are. Every prompt ends with the same instruction: do not write the code for me.

Narrow the idea 
I am a high school student in a three-week program. I am building a small app called my project for one person I know. The one job it should do: one small job.Ask me five short questions that would make this project smaller and clearer. Then give me three next steps, one at a time. Keep every answer under six lines, use plain words, and do not write the code for me.
Cut it down to three features 
My project is my project, a small app for one person I know. I have these feature ideas: my first feature, my second feature, my third feature, and some extras I have not listed yet.Help me cut this down to three features for a first version. Ask me what my one person actually needs, explain what you would drop and why, then give me the three features as short task-sized steps. Do not write the code for me.
Write the one-sentence pitch 
My project: my project, a small app for one person I know. The one job it does: one small job.Write five different one-sentence versions of this pitch. Each must say who it is for and what it does, with no hype and no adjectives that cannot be checked. Tell me which one is clearest and why. Do not write the code for me.
