One link, three testers, one fix.

Launch Day is the last day of the program. Nothing new gets built. You publish, post, and fill the final form.

What counts as launched

Three things, not a finished product.

  • A public link that opens in a private window, without signing in.
  • Three people who are not you have tried the main job.
  • You fixed one thing they found, and tested it again.

How to post it in Discord

  1. 01 Open the launch channel in Discord.
  2. 02 Post four lines: name, who it is for, what you fixed, and the link.
  3. 03 Keep the link clickable. Do not attach screenshots instead.
  4. 04 Read two other posts and try their projects.
