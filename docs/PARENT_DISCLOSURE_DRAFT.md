## The short version

- PineLaunch is a free online workspace where your teen turns their own idea into a small working website over about four weeks.
- **No accounts.** Your teen never types a name, email or password into PineLaunch.
- **The project stays on your teen's computer.** PineLaunch keeps no copy on its servers.
- **The helper is an AI, not a person.** It gives hints and asks questions. It is built not to do the project for your teen.
- **Some of your teen's words leave the computer**, only when they press a button to ask the AI for help. They go to an AI company, Anthropic, which keeps them for a time under its own rules.
- **Your teen gets 200 AI requests**, unlocked by a private access code. It costs your family nothing.
- **You can stop it at any time.** Your teen keeps everything already on their computer.

## What the beta is

Four students, all 15, use PineLaunch from **December 14, 2026 to January 31, 2027**. Each one describes an idea in their own words, gets a four-week plan with milestones, gets three starting files so they never face a blank screen, builds the project with a live preview, asks the AI helper when stuck, and downloads the finished files to keep.

It works on a laptop or desktop computer, in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

## The AI helper

## What leaves your teen's computer, and when

Nothing is sent while your teen types. Entering the access code sends only the code to PineLaunch, to check it. Anything else is sent only when your teen presses a button that uses an AI request:

| When your teen presses | What is sent |
|---|---|
| "Make my plan" | Their five idea answers: what they want to build, the problem, who would use it, the main thing it does, and their coding experience |
| "Make a new milestone map" | The same answers, and their plan |
| "Make my starter" | The same answers, their plan, and their milestones |
| A question to the helper (by December 27) | The question, the part of their code it is about, and any error message |

This goes to PineLaunch's server, which passes it to Anthropic and returns the answer. **PineLaunch's server keeps no copy**, and never sends the access code to Anthropic.

PineLaunch reminds your teen to leave out names, schools, addresses and phone numbers. Please remind them too.

## Access codes and the 200 requests

- **[PLACEHOLDER (founder or parent): who hands out the codes]** gives your teen one private code, like PL-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. It contains nothing about your teen.
